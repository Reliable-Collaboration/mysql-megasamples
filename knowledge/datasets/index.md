# datasets

## Concepts
* [AdventureWorksDW](adventureworks-dw.md) - The AdventureWorks star-schema data warehouse (31 dbo tables, dimension/fact naming), available as .bak files or as a SQLCMD install script with 30 pipe-delimited UTF-8 CSVs (~86 MB); MIT licensed.
* [AdventureWorksLT](adventureworks-lt.md) - The lightweight 12-table AdventureWorks (SalesLT schema); shipped as small version-specific .bak files (1.7-14 MB) and, only for the 2012/2008R2 releases, as a script + CSV zip (937 KB) that needs no SQL Server; MIT licensed.
* [AdventureWorks (OLTP)](adventureworks-oltp.md) - Microsoft's flagship 68-table, 5-schema OLTP sample (bicycle manufacturer); available as version-specific .bak files and as a SQLCMD install script plus 69 UTF-8 CSV files (~95 MB) that need no SQL Server to read; MIT licensed.
* [BTS Airline On-Time Performance](bts-ontime.md) - One month of the US DOT Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance table (~536k flights, 109 columns) plus the airport and carrier lookup tables; public domain.
* [Chicago crimes 2001 to present](chicago-crimes.md) - The City of Chicago's 8.6 M-row reported-crime extract plus the 434-row IUCR code lookup; a single year (2024, 259,267 rows) is proposed for the core tier.
* [Chinook](chinook.md) - Luis Rocha's digital media store sample (v1.4.5, 2024-02-12) - 11 tables, 15,607 rows, official MySQL script, MIT-licensed, rich accented Latin text.
* [Citi Bike (NYC) trip data](citibike.md) - Lyft-operated Citi Bike trip records from the public S3 bucket; the licence forbids redistributing the data as a stand-alone dataset, so it is extended tier and user-fetched only.
* [Contoso (SQLBI Contoso Data Generator V2)](contoso.md) - SQLBI's synthetic Contoso retail star schema V2 - customer, product, store, date, currencyexchange, sales, orders, orderrows - published as ready-to-use CSV sets of 10K to 100M orders under MIT; csv-100k (9.8 MB 7z) for core.
* [Dell DVD Store 3 (DS3)](dell-dvd-store.md) - Dell/VMware's open-source OLTP benchmark schema (DVD e-commerce with reviews and memberships); Small size = 20,000 customers, 12,000 orders, 10,000 products, plus 200,000 reviews; MySQL kit; GPL-2.0-or-later.
* [Divvy (Chicago) trip data](divvy.md) - City-of-Chicago-owned, Lyft-operated Divvy trip records from a public S3 bucket; the licence forbids redistributing the data as a stand-alone dataset, so it is extended tier and user-fetched only.
* [Employees (datacharmer/test_db)](employees.md) - The MySQL "Employees Sample Database" - 300,024 fabricated employees with 2.8 M salary rows; MySQL-native SQL dumps with a published SHA-256 integrity suite; CC BY-SA 3.0.
* [Enron email corpus (CMU, May 7, 2015 version)](enron.md) - About 517k real corporate emails from 150 Enron custodians, distributed by CMU as a maildir-style tree of RFC 822 files; converted to messages/recipients/mailbox tables with FULLTEXT.
* [Iris (Fisher 1936)](iris.md) - The 150-row, 4-feature iris classification table; UCI distributes it under CC BY 4.0 (licensing flag), scikit-learn/R ship a corrected copy under BSD-3; ship the corrected data with dual attribution.
* [Jaffle Shop](jaffle-shop.md) - dbt Labs' fictional jaffle (toasted sandwich) shop - classic seeds (customers 100, orders 99, payments 113) under Apache-2.0, plus the newer jafgen-generated multi-table data (1 year = 62K orders; 6 years public on S3).
* [Lahman Baseball Database (SABR, Version 2025)](lahman.md) - Sean Lahman's historical MLB statistics 1871-2025, now published by SABR as 27 CSV tables (plus Access and SQL Server forms) under CC BY-SA 3.0; sizes and encoding still to be measured because the download is a Box share.
* [Northwind](northwind.md) - Microsoft's classic 13-table trading-company sample (SQL Server 2000 era) shipped as a single 1 MB T-SQL script with all data inline; MIT licensed.
* [NYC TLC trip records](nyc-tlc.md) - New York City yellow and green taxi trip records in Parquet, plus the 265-row taxi zone lookup; one month per colour, with the yellow month in the extended tier.
* [Oracle CO (Customer Orders) sample schema](oracle-co.md) - Oracle's "modern" e-commerce sample (7 tables, 8,783 rows) with identity columns, a JSON check constraint on a BLOB, 9-digit fractional timestamps and analytic views, converted from its single 1.27 MB INSERT script.
* [Oracle HR (Human Resources) sample schema](oracle-hr.md) - The 7-table, 216-row teaching schema from Oracle's db-sample-schemas v23.3, converted from its plain INSERT scripts; the foundation OE depends on.
* [Oracle OE (Order Entry) with the archived OC, PM and IX schemas](oracle-oe-pm-ix.md) - The object-relational Order Entry schema (7 relational tables, 11,140 rows incl. 8,640 multilingual product descriptions) is worth carrying in flattened form as oracle_oe; the OC object views, PM LOB/nested-table media schema and IX AQ queues are dropped.
* [Oracle SH (Sales History) sample schema](oracle-sh.md) - Oracle's star-schema data-warehouse sample (918,843 SALES facts, 55,500 customers, 9 tables) whose v23.3 data ships as six plain CSV files loaded by SQLcl; converted with LOAD DATA, partitions/bitmap indexes/MVs/dimensions replaced.
* [Palmer Penguins](palmer-penguins.md) - 344 penguins (Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo) measured 2007-2009 at Palmer Station, Antarctica; two CSVs (tidy 8 columns, raw 17 columns) from the palmerpenguins R package; CC0.
* [pubs](pubs.md) - Microsoft's tiny 11-table publishers/authors sample (SQL Server 2000 era) shipped as a 126 KB T-SQL script with inline data; MIT licensed.
* [Sakila](sakila.md) - Oracle's DVD-rental sample database for MySQL (Version 1.5), 16 tables / 7 views / 3 procedures / 3 functions / 6 triggers, 46,268 rows, BSD-licensed SQL scripts.
* [Star Schema Benchmark (SSB) — ssb](ssb.md) - The five-table star schema (lineorder fact + customer, supplier, part, date dimensions) of O'Neil, O'Neil and Chen's Star Schema Benchmark Revision 3, generated by ssb-dbgen (a modified TPC-H dbgen) at a chosen scale factor, with the 13 queries in four flights; no data shipped.
* [Stack Exchange data dump (per-site; dba.stackexchange.com primary)](stackexchange.md) - CC BY-SA XML dump of a Stack Exchange Q&A site (Posts, Users, Comments, Votes, Badges, Tags, PostLinks, PostHistory) converted to MySQL with FULLTEXT; sourced from the last public archive.org snapshot (2024-04-02) to avoid the 2024 click-through.
* [Titanic (titanic3, Vanderbilt Biostatistics)](titanic.md) - Thomas Cason's 1,309-passenger Titanic survival dataset (14 columns) hosted by Frank Harrell's hbiostat.org under a blanket permission with acknowledgement; chosen over Kaggle's login-gated 891-row train set.
* [TPC-C (derived) — tpcc](tpc-c.md) - The nine-table order-entry OLTP schema of TPC Benchmark C populated for W warehouses by an open-source "TPC-C-like" loader (sysbench-tpcc, with HammerDB TPROC-C as the reference/fallback); no TPC software exists for TPC-C and no data is shipped.
* [TPC-DS (derived) — tpcds](tpc-ds.md) - The 24-table retail data-warehouse schema of TPC Benchmark DS (7 fact, 17 dimension tables), generated by dsdgen 2.10.0 (DuckDB port, cross-checked with tpcds-kit) at a chosen scale factor, with the 99 query templates ported to MySQL; no pre-generated data is shipped.
* [TPC-H (derived) — tpch](tpc-h.md) - The eight-table decision-support schema of TPC Benchmark H, generated at a chosen scale factor by dbgen 2.17.3 (DuckDB port, cross-checked with tpch-kit), loaded from pipe-delimited files, with the 22 validation queries ported to MySQL; no pre-generated data is shipped.
* [WideWorldImportersDW](wideworldimporters-dw.md) - The star-schema warehouse companion of WideWorldImporters (Dimension/Fact/Integration schemas, 8 dimensions, 6 facts, table names with spaces), shipped only as .bak/.bacpac (Full with columnstore/partitioning, Standard without); MIT licensed.
* [WideWorldImporters](wideworldimporters.md) - Microsoft's SQL Server 2016+ showcase OLTP sample (wholesale importer): temporal tables, memory-optimized tables, columnstore, JSON, geography, sequences, RLS; shipped only as .bak/.bacpac (Full and Standard editions), data generated by randomised T-SQL; MIT licensed with public-domain Natural Earth geodata.
* [Wikidata (candidate — not recommended; prefer Simple English Wikipedia)](wikidata.md) - CC0 structured data whose official dumps are 43-253 GB; no small official relational-friendly extract exists, so a reproducible bundle is impractical; recorded as a candidate with the extract options evaluated.
* [Simple English Wikipedia (simplewiki dump 20260901)](wikipedia-simple.md) - Current-revision article text plus MediaWiki link tables of the Simple English Wikipedia, loaded as page/revision/text/categorylinks/pagelinks/linktarget/redirect/category with FULLTEXT on wikitext.
