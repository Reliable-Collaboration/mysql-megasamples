# Terms (read 2026-09-02)
> 17 U.S.C. Sec. 105(a): "Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States Government, but the United States Government is not precluded from receiving and holding copyrights transferred to it by assignment, bequest, or otherwise."

USA.gov's guidance adds the surviving limits: third-party content on federal sites may still be protected; federal trademarks and agency logos may not be used without permission; materials may not be used "in a way that implies endorsement by a government agency, official, or employee"; and "U.S. copyright laws may not protect U.S. government works outside the country."

The On-Time Performance table is compiled by the Bureau of Transportation Statistics, an agency of the U.S. Department of Transportation, from carrier filings mandated by 14 CFR Part 234; it is a U.S. Government work.
