# What was read
The NYC Open Data FAQ page, fetched 2026-09-02.

# Relevant excerpt
> "Open Data belongs to all New Yorkers. **There are no restrictions on the use of Open Data.**"

The page also states that the NYC Open Data Law requires all "public datasets" to be published on the Open Data Portal, and links to the separate Terms of Use rather than restating them. No attribution requirement, no share-alike obligation and no API-key requirement is stated on this page.

# What it was used to decide
The permission half of [NYC Open Data terms](/licenses/nyc-open-data-terms.md), applied to [NYC TLC trip records](/datasets/nyc-tlc.md).
