# Relevant excerpt
Post date June 25, 2009. Requirements: (1) "Visually indicate that the content is from Stack Overflow or the Stack Exchange network"; (2) "Hyperlink directly to the original question"; (3) "Show the author names"; (4) "Hyperlink each author name" to the profile; links must be plain HTML to the site's domain, no URL shorteners, and must not be nofollowed.
