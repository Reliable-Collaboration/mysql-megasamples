> Question: "No additional restrictions — You may not apply legal terms or technological measures that legally restrict others from doing anything the license permits." ... "the additional terms being added by Stack Overflow are a violation of that license."
> Top answer: "This wording is not a violation of the CC BY-SA license. There are no restrictions on how you use this file. ... If you do train a large-language model, SE cannot take action against you for violating the license. However, they can opt to not give you access to further data dumps. CC BY-SA content can be gated. ... The terms of use are a separate contractual agreement and not part of the CC-licensed material."
